Revision: vibrant blue & water in motion

The revised logo is used as supplied. The new concept combines electric blue, aqua, navy and white with a water-drop product showcase, animated waves, flowing pipe illustration, product hover effects, scroll entrances and a pause-motion control. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Page content and navigation stay in the HTML, and the preview remains noindex until the approved production launch.

What has been built

  • A new homepage with product discovery, borewell services, brand names, advice, branches and FAQs.
  • A searchable catalogue and nine category guides: monoblock, openwell, borewell, industrial motors, portable pumpsets, generators, pipes and fittings, spares, and water filters.
  • A dedicated borewell service page covering the stages already listed on the business website.
  • A location filter and six business-location pages drawn from the existing ventures page, including the wholesale distribution business. This is not a claim of six retail stores.
  • An About page preserving the other listed group ventures and partnership enquiries.
  • A contact page that prepares a WhatsApp message. It collects no data on a website server, opens WhatsApp only when selected, and leaves the final send to the visitor.
  • Separate preview privacy and purchase information pages. They are not a replacement for owner-approved final policies.

The package contains 23 customer-facing pages plus these review notes. Original product images are reused by URL. The motor category uses a labelled illustration because no dedicated motor photograph was identified in the supplied catalogue. Images illustrate categories; they do not assert current model availability.

The proposed customer journey

  1. Arrive: immediately understand that A K Brothers deals in water pumps and motors in North Kerala.
  2. Explore: choose a product category or borewell service.
  3. Understand: see what site details help the team assess a requirement.
  4. Contact: call, email, prepare a WhatsApp enquiry or locate a branch.
  5. Purchase: confirm the model, price, stock and terms directly with the store.

SEO built into the concept

The pages contain crawlable HTML content and links, distinct titles and descriptions, descriptive URLs, image alternatives, canonical URL drafts and business structured data using the known Kannur contact/address. No ratings, opening hours, founding date, product prices or technical specifications have been invented.

The approach follows Google’s people-first content guidance, its guidance on navigable site structure and local business structured data documentation. These are foundations, not a ranking guarantee.

This review version deliberately has noindex metadata and a restrictive robots.txt. Its canonical drafts and sitemap-for-launch.xml describe the proposed final domain structure; they must be checked and activated only during launch. Do not submit the preview sitemap to Google. The preview social URLs also need to match the final published domain.

Before replacing the live site

  1. Confirm core facts: trading name, relationship to AK Holdings, founding year, current branches, exact addresses, opening hours, correct central number and email.
  2. Confirm the offer: stocked brands and models, dealer status, service territory, borewell scope, quotation exclusions, delivery/collection, warranty and returns.
  3. Choose the Wix implementation route: this is a static website preview. It is not a set of editable Wix Editor sections. Rebuild the approved design within the current editor, or plan a deliberate move to a code-managed frontend. Preserve the current site until that decision is made.
  4. Add useful evidence: branch/team photographs, current product specifications and PDFs, completed work, genuine customer reviews with permission. Do not invent testimonials.
  5. Prepare search migration: retain the domain, preserve verification tags, review existing indexed pages, apply agreed redirects, update canonical/social URLs and sitemap, validate structured data, and remove preview indexing blocks from the final production site only.
  6. Check business listings and measurement: align verified business name/address/phone with Google Business Profiles; measure calls, WhatsApp opens, category visits and branch visits after configuring the chosen analytics and privacy controls.
  7. Release and verify: test forms/contact routes without sending test enquiries, every old redirect, mobile navigation, image loading, HTTP status codes and performance. Submit the final sitemap through the existing verified Search Console property if accessible.

What has and has not been verified

The review used the public home, products, ventures, about and contact pages, live browser checks, and read-only Wix site/SEO data. PageSpeed/Core Web Vitals, Google rankings, Search Console performance and conversions were not measured. A hidden or secondary static page /ak-brothers-kannur was discovered through Wix; its current content must be reviewed before redirecting it.

No original page, SEO tag, domain, product or account setting was changed. No test message or enquiry was sent. The prepared website has no checkout or payment collection.
