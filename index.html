A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Useful answers.

Ask us something else ↗
How do I choose the right water pump?+

Start with the water source, pumping water level, delivery height, pipe length, water requirement and electricity supply. Share what you know with our team; you do not need to know the model before getting in touch.

Do you help with borewell drilling and installation?+

Yes. The existing A K Brothers website lists drilling, testing, pump selection, installation and commissioning as part of its borewell service. Contact our team to confirm availability, scope and a quotation for your location.

Can I buy a pump online?+

Purchases are arranged through our stores. Use this website to explore categories and discuss your requirement. Our team can confirm the model, price, availability and collection or site-service arrangements before purchase.

What should I send for a replacement pump or motor?+

Send a clear photograph of the nameplate, the existing equipment and the connection or mounting arrangement, along with the problem you are experiencing. Our team can then discuss the next step.
